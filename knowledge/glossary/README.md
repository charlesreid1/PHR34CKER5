# GLOSSARY

Terms of art. Skim this file the first time you're lost.

## Signaling
- **MF** — Multi-Frequency. In-band trunk signaling; 6 tones, pairs of 2.
- **DTMF** — Dual-Tone Multi-Frequency. What your handset keypad sends.
- **SF** — Single Frequency. 2600 Hz supervision.
- **CCIS** — Common Channel Interoffice Signaling. Out-of-band signaling,
  predecessor to SS7.
- **SS7** — Signaling System No. 7. Modern OOB signaling network.
- **ANI** — Automatic Number Identification. The line the call originated
  from, as the network sees it (predates Caller ID).
- **ANAC** — Automatic Number Announcement Circuit. Dial it, it reads back
  your own ANI. Every CO had one at a different number.

## Corporate
- **AT&T** — American Telephone & Telegraph. Ma Bell.
- **BOC** — Bell Operating Company. Local branches of the pre-1984 Bell
  System.
- **RBOC** — Regional BOC. The seven "Baby Bells" formed by the 1984
  divestiture: NYNEX, Bell Atlantic, BellSouth, Ameritech, Southwestern
  Bell, US West, Pacific Telesis.
- **ILEC** — Incumbent Local Exchange Carrier (post-1996 term).
- **CLEC** — Competitive LEC.
- **IXC** — Interexchange Carrier. Long-distance provider.

## Geography
- **LATA** — Local Access and Transport Area. Divestiture-era boundary
  between local and long-distance.
- **NPA** — Numbering Plan Area. What you call an area code.
- **NXX** — Central office prefix (the middle three digits).
- **POI** — Point of Interface between carriers.

## Boxes (a partial zoo)
- **Blue** — MF trunk signaling.
- **Red** — coin-deposit ACTS tones.
- **Green** — operator coin collect/return.
- **Black** — bypass called-party billing (older step-by-step trick).
- **Beige** — a lineman's handset. Alligator clips onto a residential
  pair.
- **Cheese** — ties two lines together for conferencing / privacy.
- **Silver** — extends DTMF keypad to A/B/C/D (autovon tones).
