# ESS — ELECTRONIC SWITCHING SYSTEMS

The generations of AT&T / Western Electric central office switches that
replaced electromechanical crossbar and step-by-step exchanges.

## The lineage
- **1ESS** (1965) — first stored-program-control switch. Reed relays in the
  fabric, magnetic-core memory for program store. Analog voice, digital
  control.
- **1AESS** (1976) — faster, higher capacity 1ESS.
- **2ESS** (1970) — suburban / smaller-office variant.
- **3ESS** (1976) — for really small offices.
- **4ESS** (1976) — the *toll* switch. Handled long-distance trunking.
  The first to see wide CCIS deployment.
- **5ESS** (1982, Lucent) — fully digital, distributed control. Ran most of
  American local switching from the mid-1980s onward. Still in service in
  places.
- **DMS-100** (Northern Telecom) — the Canadian answer, widely deployed in
  the US too.

## Why this file exists
Understanding what switch was on the other end of your call is half of
understanding what tricks were possible on it. 1ESS talked MF on trunks;
4ESS with CCIS 6 already had SS7's ancestor; 5ESS was fully OOB from day
one.
