# Zines — landmark articles reference

A curated list of the phreak-relevant articles the corpus most often
cites, with mirror URLs. This is the "which file do I fetch" lookup;
the biographical / cultural framing lives in
[[zines/README]].

Each entry maps to a `bibliography` record in the KR — cite `phrack-
33-9`, `phrack-44-19`, `2600-autumn-1990`, etc.

## 2600: The Hacker Quarterly — landmarks

- **Vol. 7 No. 3 (Autumn 1990) — "Converting a Tone Dialer into a Red
  Box"**, Noah Clayton. The original 43-141 crystal-swap procedure.
  Bib: `2600-autumn-1990`.
- **Vol. 10 No. 4 (Winter 1993-94) — Red Box refresher**. The article
  that repopularized the ACTS timing patterns for the mid-90s scene.
- **Vol. 11 No. 1 (Spring 1994) — "The COCOT Files"**. Default admin
  passwords + service-mode entry for Elcotel, Protel, Intellicall.
- **Vol. 14 No. 3 (Autumn 1997) — "Inside a Meridian"**. Zine-level SL-1
  craft session walkthrough. Bib: `2600-autumn-1997-meridian`.

Meetings first Friday every month; still publishing.

## TAP (Technological American Party) — 1971-1984

Mimeographed newsletter, phreaking-first. Full run archived at
`http://www.textfiles.com/magazines/TAP/`.

TAP predated the record-numbering discipline of Phrack, so citations
are by **issue number + article title**. Landmark issues:

- **Issues 1-15 (1971-1973)** — Under the YIPL banner. Blue-box
  schematics, red-box origins, coin-return tricks.
- **Issue 20** — Bell Labs internal-directory leak
- **Issue 40s** — CN/A bureau numbers by region (public-domain by
  decades; era-authentic list is here rather than being republished by
  the corpus).
- **Issue 66 (1980)** — Final YIPL/TAP transition-era issue before the
  1984 fold.

TAP was influential culturally: it was the *phreak* zine before hacker
was a word. Anyone building a period-authentic 1975-1983 CTF puzzle
should have a TAP issue's schematics on the wall.

## Adjacent scene publications

- **NIA (Network Information Access)** — smaller, more focused, mostly
  operator/OSS material.
- **LOD/H Technical Journal** — Legion of Doom / Hackers. Only 4 issues
  1987-1989 but each is a small book.
- **cDc (Cult of the Dead Cow) text files** — culturally central,
  technically varied; Back Orifice released as t-file 173.
- **Anarchist Cookbook** — culturally adjacent, technically unreliable.
  Don't cite it for anything phone-related.
